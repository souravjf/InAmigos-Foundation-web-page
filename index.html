About Us

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla and is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. The foundation operates across states including Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh, addressing critical societal issues through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers. It holds 80G, 12A, CSR-1, NITI Aayog and IAF ISO 9001:2015 certifications, ensuring transparency and accountability in every initiative.

Our Projects

Project SEVA

Providing freshly cooked, hygienic food and clothing to homeless families and underprivileged communities across India.

Project Bachpanshala

Bridging educational gaps by training underprivileged children in digital literacy, life skills and school education support.

Project Udaan

Empowering women and elderly community members through skill development, dialogue and support to build self reliant communities.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental conservation through tree plantation drives, clean water advocacy and sustainability awareness programs.

Project Jeev

Protecting and rescuing street animals in need while spreading awareness about animal welfare and care.

Project Vikas

Enhancing employability of youth and spreading public health awareness through structured community programs.

Our Social Impact

Every initiative we run is designed to bridge the gap between resource abundance and community deprivation, bringing effective solutions straight to the grassroots level.

  • Over 50,000 meals and clothing items distributed to the underprivileged
  • Over 30,000 interns and youth volunteers empowered nationwide
  • 28 states across India reached through our programs
  • 28 dedicated volunteers working on ground level initiatives
